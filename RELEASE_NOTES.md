Local Desk 1.0.0 — standalone macOS desktop chat.

- Apple Silicon (arm64), macOS 26+.
- Bundled Python, Qt, MLX/Metal, document readers and Apple Vision OCR helper.
- Fast/thinking modes, streaming, saved chats, document attachment and exports.
- Built-in model downloads and connection of existing weight folders.
- Qwen3.5 9B, Gemma 4 12B OptiQ, Qwen3.8 27B; weights downloaded separately.

Installation: open the DMG and drag Local Desk.app to Applications. Python/Homebrew are not required.

This build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. Gatekeeper may require manual approval on another Mac. Do not disable macOS security protections.
